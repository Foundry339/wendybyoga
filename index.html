About Wendy

I am a Connecticut-based yoga instructor dedicated to helping students of all levels find balance on and off the mat. Whether you're brand new to yoga or a seasoned practitioner, I meet students exactly where they are.

My approach blends mindful breathwork, alignment-focused movement, and a genuinely warm teaching style. Sessions are never rushed and never one-size-fits-all. Every private session or pop-up class is shaped around the people in the room.

What sets my offerings apart is flexibility: book a private one-on-one session tailored entirely to your goals, or join me in one of my pop-up yoga classes held at parks, studios, and community spaces throughout Connecticut. Either way, you'll leave feeling more balanced than when you walked in.

Class Types

Wendy offers four distinct class styles for private sessions and pop-up classes around Connecticut — choose the one that fits where you are today.

Flow Yoga

A dynamic, breath-linked sequence that moves steadily from pose to pose. Great for building strength, coordination, and a meditative sense of rhythm.

Yin Yoga

A slow, quiet practice of long-held, passive poses that target the deep connective tissues. Ideal for releasing tension and calming an overactive mind.

Power Yoga

An energetic, strength-building practice that turns up the intensity. Expect a challenging, sweat-inducing flow for students who want a full-body workout.

Gentle Yoga

A soft, accessible practice with modifications for every body. Perfect for beginners, older adults, or anyone easing back into movement.
