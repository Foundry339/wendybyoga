About Me

At the heart of my work is a desire to create intentional spaces where BIPOC can experience yoga as a practice of connection, belonging, and honoring the body. I believe we deserve spaces where we feel welcomed, seen, and free to simply be. My mission is to create yoga experiences where people can feel at home in their bodies, connect with community, and explore their practice without feeling like they have to fit a certain mold.

For me, yoga has always been about more than movement. It has given me space to slow down, reconnect with my body, quiet the noise, and cultivate a deeper connection between mind, body, and spirit. After experiencing that sense of connection through my own practice, I felt called to create that experience for others.

I am a 200-hour certified yoga teacher with 6 years of teaching experience. My approach is rooted in mindful movement, intentional breathing, body awareness, and meeting students exactly where they are. Whether you're stepping onto a yoga mat for the first time or looking to deepen an established practice, I believe there is no single way to practice yoga, and no expectation that your practice should look like anyone else's.

Sessions are never rushed and never one-size-fits-all.

My teaching creates room to explore, listen inward, and discover what your body and mind need in the present moment. Through Vinyasa flow classes, private sessions, pop-ups, and community experiences, I aim to make yoga feel approachable, intentional, and meaningful.

Whether you're looking to move, breathe, reconnect, or simply give yourself a moment to pause, I invite you to come as you are.

Class Types

Distinct class styles for private sessions and pop-up classes around Connecticut. Choose the one that fits where you are today.

Flow Yoga

A dynamic, breath-linked sequence that moves steadily from pose to pose. Great for building strength, coordination, and a meditative sense of rhythm.

Yin Yoga

A slow, quiet practice of long-held, passive poses that target the deep connective tissues. Ideal for releasing tension and calming an overactive mind.

Intensity

Power Yoga

An energetic, strength-building practice that turns up the intensity. Expect a challenging, sweat-inducing flow for students who want a full-body workout.

Gentle Yoga

A soft, accessible practice with modifications for every body. Perfect for beginners, older adults, or anyone easing back into movement.

Why Rosa Parks Did Yoga

Scholar Stephanie Y. Evans discovered that Parks not only practiced yoga but taught it, and makes the case for rest and collective care as what sustained Black women leaders.
