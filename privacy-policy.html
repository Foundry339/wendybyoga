Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Wendy B. Yoga ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is intentionally simple and applies only to this website, wendybyoga.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide, including your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We do not require an account to use this site, and we do not knowingly collect payment or financial information through it.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information submitted through the contact form only to respond to your inquiry, such as scheduling a private session or answering a question about a class. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Formspree, to process and deliver contact form submissions.
  • Google Fonts, to load the typefaces used on this site.
  • Font Awesome, to load icons used on this site.

These services may receive standard technical information, such as your browser type or IP address, as part of normal web requests. Each provider has its own privacy policy governing how it handles this information.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies for advertising or tracking. Some third-party services listed above may set minimal technical cookies as part of their normal operation.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through this site. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out using the contact form on the Contact page.
